## The core factors read for a career question
A career reading rests on four things examined together: the 10th house and any planets in it, the 10th house lord and where it sits, the 10th house counted from the Moon as a second opinion, and the currently running Mahadasha and Antardasha. The 1st house lord is added as a measure of overall personal capacity, and the 6th house is consulted for service, employment and the ability to withstand competition. A single strong factor is not enough: a careful reading looks for agreement between at least two of these before committing to a direction.

## Saturn and career
Saturn is the karaka, or natural significator, of work, service, and career built through sustained effort. A strong Saturn influencing the 10th house is read as a career that grows slowly and holds, typically in administration, engineering, law, government, infrastructure, mining, labour-intensive industry, or any field where seniority is earned over decades. A Saturn Mahadasha is traditionally read as a period for consolidation, restructuring and disciplined effort rather than for rapid expansion or risky moves. Saturn afflicting the 10th is read as delay and repeated obstacles rather than as failure, and classical practice expects results to arrive later than hoped rather than not at all.

## Sun, Mercury and Jupiter in professional matters
A strong Sun influencing the 10th gives authority, visibility and standing, and supports government service, administration, politics, medicine and leadership roles. Mercury supports analysis, communication, trade, accounting, writing, software and anything requiring quick intellectual handling of detail. Jupiter supports teaching, law, advisory work, finance, publishing and counselling, and its aspect on the 10th is read as protective of reputation. Mars supports engineering, defence, sport, surgery and competitive fields. Venus supports design, fashion, entertainment, hospitality and any field where aesthetic judgement is the product.

## Rahu, Ketu and unconventional career paths
Rahu influencing the 10th house is a classical indicator of unconventional, foreign-linked or technology-driven career success, often arriving faster than expected and requiring constant reinvention to hold. Rahu is associated with modern fields that did not exist a generation earlier. Ketu influencing the 10th is read as deep specialisation combined with a lack of interest in the recognition that specialisation brings, and often shows in research, analysis and behind-the-scenes technical roles.

## Job change, switching and starting a business
The question of whether to change jobs is read from the 10th house for the work itself, the 6th house for employment and service, the 11th for whether income actually improves, and the running Dasha for whether the timing supports change at all. A Dasha lord connected to the 10th or 11th during a period of active Jupiter or Saturn transit over those houses is the classical combination for a beneficial change. Independent business is read from the 7th house alongside the 10th, since business depends on dealings with others, and a strong 3rd house is looked for as the capacity for self-driven effort without external structure.

## Career growth versus career change
Growth within an existing role is read primarily from the 10th and 11th houses and from whether the current Dasha lord supports them. A genuine change of direction is read from movement involving the 10th lord, the 9th house when the change is toward one's own dharma or calling, and the 12th when it involves relocation or foreign work. A period that supports growth does not necessarily support change, and the distinction matters when advising: the same chart can favour staying and progressing while disfavouring a move in the same year.

## Timing professional results
Career results are timed by combining Dasha with transit. Jupiter transiting the 10th, 11th or the 2nd is traditionally watched for professional expansion and income. Saturn transiting the 10th is read as a period of heavy responsibility that consolidates standing rather than lifting it quickly. The classical caution is that a good transit during an unsupportive Dasha produces a smaller result than the transit alone would suggest, so both must agree before a strong timing statement is justified.

## The limits of a career reading
Astrology describes tendency, timing and the shape of a period; it does not identify a specific employer, a specific salary figure, or guarantee an outcome. A responsible reading frames career guidance as an indication to be weighed alongside skills, market conditions and the person's own judgement, and is explicit that the chart shows what is supported rather than what is certain.
