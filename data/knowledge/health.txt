## The houses and factors read for health
Health is read from the 1st house and its lord, which represent the body and its overall constitution, and from the 6th house, which governs disease, recovery and the capacity to resist illness. The 8th house governs longevity and chronic or sudden conditions, and the 12th governs hospitalisation, rest and depletion. The Moon is examined for mental and emotional wellbeing, and the Sun for vitality. A strong 1st house lord with a supportive 6th is the classical picture of a robust constitution that recovers well.

## What each planet governs in the body
The Sun governs the bones, heart and eyes, and general vitality. The Moon governs bodily fluids, the stomach, the mind and sleep. Mars governs blood, muscles, inflammation, accidents and surgery. Mercury governs the nervous system, skin and speech. Jupiter governs the liver, fat metabolism and general growth. Venus governs the reproductive system, kidneys and the sense of taste. Saturn governs the joints, teeth, chronic and slow-developing conditions, and ageing. Rahu is associated with conditions that are hard to diagnose, and Ketu with conditions whose cause is obscure.

## Mental and emotional wellbeing
The Moon and the 4th house together are the primary indicators of peace of mind. An afflicted Moon, particularly when in contact with Saturn, Rahu or Ketu, is traditionally read as a tendency toward anxiety, low mood or emotional instability during periods ruled by those planets. Mercury is examined for the nervous system and clarity of thought. Classical practice reads these as indications of tendency and of periods requiring extra care, never as a diagnosis.

## Sade Sati, Dasha periods and health timing
The peak phase of Sade Sati, when Saturn transits the natal Moon sign, is traditionally watched for fatigue, low mood and strain on health. Mahadashas and Antardashas of planets connected to the 6th, 8th or 12th houses are read as periods requiring more attention to wellbeing. These indications describe a window in which care is warranted; they do not predict a specific illness, and presenting them as though they did is a misuse of the technique.

## The boundary that must not be crossed
Astrology does not diagnose illness, does not identify a condition, does not predict lifespan, and is never a substitute for a doctor. An astrological health indication points at most to an area deserving attention and a period during which extra care is sensible. When a person describes a symptom, an active health problem, or distress, the correct response is to encourage them to consult a qualified medical professional, and to decline to offer an astrological explanation of a medical condition. Questions about longevity should not be answered with a timeframe under any circumstances.

## Responding to a person in distress
If a person expresses hopelessness, self-harm, or severe distress, astrology is not the appropriate response. The correct response is to acknowledge what they have said with warmth, to say clearly that speaking to a mental health professional or a crisis helpline would help more than a chart can, and not to offer a reading that frames their distress as a planetary period they must simply endure. Framing genuine suffering as fated is harmful and is not supported by classical practice, which consistently treats remedy, effort and free will as real.
