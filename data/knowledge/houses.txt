## The 1st house (Lagna or Ascendant) — self, body, vitality
The 1st house is the sign rising at the moment of birth and represents the physical body, constitution, temperament and the overall direction of life. The strength of its lord is one of the most important single indicators in a chart. Every other house is counted from here, so an error in birth time shifts the entire house structure. Planets in the 1st house colour the personality directly.

## The 2nd house — wealth, speech, family
The 2nd house governs accumulated wealth, savings, speech, food and the immediate family a person is born into. It is the house of money already earned and held, as distinct from the 11th which is money coming in. Afflictions here are traditionally read as strain on savings or on family harmony. Jupiter or Venus here is considered supportive of both wealth and speech.

## The 3rd house — courage, siblings, effort
The 3rd house covers courage, initiative, younger siblings, short journeys, communication and self-driven effort. It is an upachaya house, meaning even malefic planets placed here tend to improve in result over time. Mars or Saturn in the 3rd is considered good for sustained personal drive and for the willingness to keep pushing after setbacks.

## The 4th house — home, mother, inner peace
The 4th house governs the mother, home, land and property, vehicles, early education and, above all, peace of mind. A strong 4th house is read as emotional security and a stable base. Questions about relocation, buying property, or feeling unsettled are read from the 4th house and from the Moon together.

## The 5th house — intelligence, children, romance
The 5th house covers intelligence, creativity, romance, speculation, children and purva punya, the merit carried forward. It is the primary house for love affairs, as distinct from the 7th which governs formalised partnership. Jupiter's influence on the 5th is the classical indicator for children. Speculative gains and creative work are also read here.

## The 6th house — obstacles, health, service, competition
The 6th house governs disease, debts, enemies, litigation, daily service and competition. It is an upachaya house, so malefics here often do well: they are read as the strength to overcome obstacles rather than as the obstacles themselves. Saturn or Mars in the 6th is traditionally read as the capacity to outlast competitors and sustain demanding work. Chronic health matters are examined here alongside the 1st house lord.

## The 7th house — marriage, partnership, contracts
The 7th house governs the spouse, marriage, business partnership and all formal one-to-one contracts. It sits directly opposite the 1st house, which is why partnership is read as the complement to the self. The three standard factors examined for marriage are the 7th lord's placement, the condition of Venus, and whether Jupiter aspects the 7th. Afflictions to the 7th are read as needing care in partner selection and in timing, not as denial of marriage.

## The 8th house — transformation, longevity, hidden matters
The 8th house governs longevity, sudden events, inheritance, joint finances, insurance, occult study and deep transformation. It is a dusthana, a difficult house, but it is also the house of research and of anything requiring one to go beneath the surface. Money arriving through a spouse, an inheritance or an investment is read here rather than in the 2nd.

## The 9th house — fortune, dharma, higher learning
The 9th house governs fortune, the father, the guru, long journeys, higher education, philosophy and religious inclination. It is considered the single most auspicious house in the chart. A strong 9th lord is read as sustained good fortune and as support arriving at the right moment without being sought.

## The 10th house — career, status, public life
The 10th house governs profession, reputation, authority and public standing. It is the primary house for career questions and is read alongside its lord, any planets occupying it, the 10th house counted from the Moon, and the currently running Dasha. The 10th is an upachaya house, so effort here compounds over time. The Sun, Mercury, Jupiter and Saturn are each considered capable of giving good professional results from the 10th, in their own respective fields.

## The 11th house — gains, income, networks
The 11th house governs income, gains, elder siblings, friendships and the fulfilment of desires. It is the house of money flowing in, as opposed to the 2nd which holds what has been kept. All planets, including malefics, are traditionally considered capable of giving gains from the 11th. A strong 11th alongside a strong 10th is the classical combination for professional income growth.

## The 12th house — loss, expenditure, foreign lands, moksha
The 12th house governs expenditure, loss, isolation, foreign residence, hospitals, sleep and spiritual liberation. It is a dusthana, but it is also the house of foreign settlement and of genuine spiritual practice. Planets here are read as energy spent outward rather than held. A strong 12th with a strong 9th is a classical indicator of living or working abroad.

## Kendra, Trikona, Dusthana and Upachaya
Kendras (houses 1, 4, 7 and 10) are the pillars of the chart and give strength and stability. Trikonas (1, 5 and 9) are the houses of fortune and dharma. Dusthanas (6, 8 and 12) are the difficult houses. Upachaya houses (3, 6, 10 and 11) improve with time and effort, which is why malefic planets placed there are often read positively. A planet that rules both a kendra and a trikona for a given ascendant is called a yogakaraka and is treated as a strong benefic for that chart.

## How houses are counted from a reference point other than the Ascendant
A house can be counted from any point, not only the Ascendant. Counting the 10th house from the Moon gives a second reading of career, and counting the 7th from Venus gives a second reading of marriage. When both the Ascendant-based and Moon-based readings agree, the indication is considered stronger. This is why a careful reading checks the same theme from more than one reference point before committing to it.
