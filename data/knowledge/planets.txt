## Sun (Surya) — the soul and the self
The Sun signifies the soul (atma), vitality, father, authority, government, bones and the eyes. A well-placed Sun gives confidence, leadership and recognition; an afflicted Sun can show ego conflict, trouble with authority figures, or a strained relationship with the father. The Sun is exalted in Aries, debilitated in Libra, and rules Leo. It is considered strong in the 10th house, which is why a strong Sun is read as a marker of professional visibility. The Sun takes about one month to cross each sign.

## Moon (Chandra) — the mind and emotions
The Moon signifies the mind (manas), emotions, the mother, memory, fluids in the body and the general sense of comfort and belonging. The Moon's sign is the rashi that most Indian astrology uses to describe a person day to day, and the Moon's nakshatra determines the starting point of the Vimshottari Dasha cycle. The Moon is exalted in Taurus, debilitated in Scorpio, and rules Cancer. A waxing Moon is considered stronger than a waning one. Emotional questions, mental peace and matters concerning the mother are read from the Moon and from the 4th house.

## Mars (Mangal) — energy, courage and conflict
Mars signifies energy, courage, siblings (especially younger ones), property, surgery, accidents, competition and physical drive. Mars rules Aries and Scorpio, is exalted in Capricorn and debilitated in Cancer. Mars is a natural malefic: it gives drive and decisiveness but can also bring haste, anger and disputes. In career matters Mars supports engineering, defence, sports, surgery and any field needing physical or competitive stamina. Mars aspects the 4th, 7th and 8th houses from where it sits.

## Mercury (Budha) — intellect and communication
Mercury signifies intelligence, speech, analysis, trade, writing, mathematics and the nervous system. Mercury rules Gemini and Virgo, is exalted in Virgo and debilitated in Pisces. Mercury takes on the character of whichever planet it sits with, so its results depend heavily on its company. Strong Mercury supports careers in software, accounting, writing, teaching, media and business. Mercury retrograde periods are traditionally read as better for revision, review and renegotiation than for signing new agreements.

## Jupiter (Guru or Brihaspati) — wisdom, expansion and grace
Jupiter signifies wisdom, teachers, children, wealth, dharma, higher learning and good fortune. Jupiter rules Sagittarius and Pisces, is exalted in Cancer and debilitated in Capricorn. Jupiter is the greatest natural benefic and its aspect on a house is traditionally considered protective. Jupiter aspects the 5th, 7th and 9th houses from itself. Jupiter takes roughly one year per sign, so Jupiter transits mark year-long themes. Jupiter's influence on the 7th house is a classical marriage indicator.

## Venus (Shukra) — love, comfort and beauty
Venus signifies love, marriage, beauty, art, vehicles, luxury, refinement and the spouse, especially in a male chart. Venus rules Taurus and Libra, is exalted in Pisces and debilitated in Virgo. Venus is a natural benefic and the primary significator, or karaka, for marriage and relationships. Strong Venus supports careers in design, fashion, entertainment, hospitality and anything requiring aesthetic judgement. Venus afflicted by Mars or Rahu is classically read as turbulence in relationships rather than as their absence.

## Saturn (Shani) — discipline, delay and endurance
Saturn signifies discipline, hard work, delay, longevity, service, old age, and lessons learned slowly. Saturn rules Capricorn and Aquarius, is exalted in Libra and debilitated in Aries. Saturn is the great teacher: it rarely gives quickly, but what it gives tends to last. Saturn aspects the 3rd, 7th and 10th houses from itself. Saturn takes about two and a half years per sign, which is why Sade Sati, Saturn transiting the 12th, 1st and 2nd from the natal Moon, lasts roughly seven and a half years. A Saturn period is read as a time for restructuring and patient effort rather than rapid expansion.

## Rahu — ambition, obsession and the unconventional
Rahu is the north lunar node, a shadow planet with no physical body. Rahu signifies ambition, foreign lands, technology, sudden rise, obsession and unconventional paths. Rahu amplifies whatever house and sign it occupies. It is associated with material hunger and with results that arrive suddenly and can leave as suddenly. Rahu is always exactly 180 degrees from Ketu and is always retrograde in its mean motion. Rahu in the 10th house is a classical indicator of unconventional or foreign-linked career success.

## Ketu — detachment, liberation and the past
Ketu is the south lunar node, opposite Rahu. Ketu signifies detachment, spirituality, past-life skill, research, isolation and moksha. Where Rahu grasps, Ketu releases. A strong Ketu can give deep specialised skill that seems to arrive without effort, alongside little interest in the worldly rewards of that skill. Ketu in the 7th house is traditionally read as detachment in partnership matters rather than as denial of partnership.

## Natural benefics, natural malefics and functional roles
Jupiter, Venus, a waxing Moon and an unafflicted Mercury are natural benefics. The Sun, Mars, Saturn, Rahu, Ketu and a waning Moon are natural malefics. This classification is only a starting point: what matters more is the functional role a planet plays for a particular ascendant, which depends on which houses it rules. A natural malefic that rules a trikona for a given ascendant can behave as a strong benefic in that chart, and a natural benefic ruling difficult houses can behave less helpfully.
