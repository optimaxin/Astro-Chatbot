## Aries (Mesha) — cardinal fire, ruled by Mars
Aries is the first sign, cardinal and fiery, ruled by Mars. It gives initiative, directness, physical courage and impatience. The Sun is exalted in Aries and Saturn is debilitated there. Aries ascendants tend to start things readily and lose interest once the challenge is gone. In career terms Aries favours independent, competitive and physically demanding work.

## Taurus (Vrishabha) — fixed earth, ruled by Venus
Taurus is fixed and earthy, ruled by Venus. It gives steadiness, patience, an appreciation of comfort and a strong resistance to being rushed. The Moon is exalted in Taurus. Taurus placements are read as slow to change but very durable once committed. Finance, food, art and land-related work suit this sign.

## Gemini (Mithuna) — mutable air, ruled by Mercury
Gemini is mutable and airy, ruled by Mercury. It gives curiosity, versatility, quick communication and a tendency to scatter attention across too many interests. Gemini supports writing, teaching, trade, media and technology. Its weakness is follow-through rather than ability.

## Cancer (Karka) — cardinal water, ruled by the Moon
Cancer is cardinal and watery, ruled by the Moon. It gives emotional depth, protectiveness, memory and attachment to home and family. Jupiter is exalted in Cancer and Mars is debilitated there. Cancer placements read as nurturing and security-seeking, and they take personal matters personally.

## Leo (Simha) — fixed fire, ruled by the Sun
Leo is fixed and fiery, ruled by the Sun. It gives dignity, generosity, a need for recognition and natural authority. Leo placements want to be seen doing something worthwhile. Leadership, performance, politics and any visible public role suit this sign.

## Virgo (Kanya) — mutable earth, ruled by Mercury
Virgo is mutable and earthy, ruled by Mercury, and is the one sign where Mercury is both ruler and exalted. Venus is debilitated in Virgo. Virgo gives analysis, precision, service-mindedness and a critical eye that can turn inward as self-criticism. It supports medicine, accounting, quality work, editing, software and anything where detail matters.

## Libra (Tula) — cardinal air, ruled by Venus
Libra is cardinal and airy, ruled by Venus. It gives diplomacy, a sense of fairness, aesthetic judgement and difficulty committing to a decision. Saturn is exalted in Libra and the Sun is debilitated there. Libra supports law, design, counselling, partnership-based business and negotiation.

## Scorpio (Vrishchika) — fixed water, ruled by Mars
Scorpio is fixed and watery, ruled by Mars and associated with Ketu. It gives intensity, secrecy, research capacity and the ability to survive transformation. The Moon is debilitated in Scorpio. Scorpio placements read as private, determined and uninterested in surface explanations. Research, investigation, surgery, occult study and crisis work suit this sign.

## Sagittarius (Dhanu) — mutable fire, ruled by Jupiter
Sagittarius is mutable and fiery, ruled by Jupiter. It gives optimism, a philosophical outlook, love of travel and bluntness. Sagittarius supports teaching, law, higher education, publishing and advisory work. Its weakness is over-extension and promising more than can be delivered.

## Capricorn (Makara) — cardinal earth, ruled by Saturn
Capricorn is cardinal and earthy, ruled by Saturn. It gives ambition, discipline, patience with long timelines and a practical unwillingness to waste effort. Mars is exalted in Capricorn and Jupiter is debilitated there. Capricorn supports administration, engineering, government and any career built slowly over decades.

## Aquarius (Kumbha) — fixed air, ruled by Saturn
Aquarius is fixed and airy, ruled by Saturn and associated with Rahu. It gives originality, social conscience, detachment and stubbornness about principles. Aquarius supports technology, research, social organisations and large systems. Its placements are read as concerned with groups and ideas more than with individuals.

## Pisces (Meena) — mutable water, ruled by Jupiter
Pisces is mutable and watery, ruled by Jupiter. It gives compassion, imagination, spiritual receptivity and a tendency to avoid hard boundaries. Venus is exalted in Pisces and Mercury is debilitated there. Pisces supports healing, art, music, charity and spiritual work. Its difficulty is with practical structure rather than with sensitivity.

## Sign qualities and elements as a reading tool
Signs are grouped by element — fire (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius), earth (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn), air (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius) and water (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces) — and by quality: cardinal or movable (Aries, Cancer, Libra, Capricorn) which initiate, fixed (Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius) which sustain, and mutable or dual (Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces) which adapt. A chart weighted toward fixed signs resists change; one weighted toward mutable signs adapts easily but settles slowly. Reading the balance of elements and qualities across the whole chart is often more informative than any single placement.

## Exaltation and debilitation summary
Sun is exalted in Aries and debilitated in Libra. Moon is exalted in Taurus and debilitated in Scorpio. Mars is exalted in Capricorn and debilitated in Cancer. Mercury is exalted in Virgo and debilitated in Pisces. Jupiter is exalted in Cancer and debilitated in Capricorn. Venus is exalted in Pisces and debilitated in Virgo. Saturn is exalted in Libra and debilitated in Aries. A debilitated planet is not simply bad: cancellation of debilitation, called neecha bhanga, occurs under specific conditions and can turn such a placement into a source of unusual strength.
