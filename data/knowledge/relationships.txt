## The core factors read for marriage
Marriage is read from the 7th house and its lord, from Venus as the natural significator of marriage, and from Jupiter, which is additionally examined in a woman's chart as the significator of the husband. The 2nd house is consulted for family life after marriage, the 11th for the fulfilment of the desire itself, and the 8th for the durability and shared resources of the union. The Navamsa chart, or D9, is examined alongside the birth chart and is considered the more reliable of the two for marriage specifically: a 7th house that looks weak in the D1 but strong in the D9 is read far more favourably than the reverse.

## Love relationships versus marriage
The 5th house governs romance, attraction and love affairs; the 7th governs formal commitment and partnership. A chart can show a strong 5th and a difficult 7th, which is classically read as an easy romantic life that struggles to formalise, and the reverse pattern is read as a marriage arranged or arrived at without a long courtship. When someone asks about a specific relationship becoming a marriage, the link between the 5th and 7th houses and their lords is the first thing examined.

## Timing of marriage
Marriage timing is read by combining the Dasha with transits. The classical indications are a Mahadasha or Antardasha of the 7th lord, of Venus, of a planet sitting in the 7th house, or of the lord of the 2nd or 11th, together with Jupiter transiting the 7th house or the 7th from the Moon. Saturn's influence on the 7th is the usual indication of delay rather than of denial. Traditional practice is firm that delay and denial are different verdicts, and that most charts showing a "difficult" 7th are describing timing and adjustment, not absence of marriage.

## Venus and the quality of relationships
Venus describes how a person loves and what they find valuable in a partner. Venus in a friendly sign and unafflicted is read as ease and warmth in relationships. Venus with Mars is read as passion alongside friction; Venus with Saturn as seriousness, loyalty and slowness to open up; Venus with Rahu as intense, unconventional or sudden attractions; Venus with Ketu as detachment or dissatisfaction despite outward comfort. These are read as descriptions of texture, not as verdicts on whether a relationship will succeed.

## Compatibility and Guna Milan
Ashtakoot Guna Milan compares the two birth nakshatras across eight factors worth a total of 36 points: varna 1, vashya 2, tara 3, yoni 4, graha maitri 5, gana 6, bhakoot 7 and nadi 8. A score of 18 or above is conventionally treated as acceptable and 24 or above as good. Nadi carries the most weight, and a shared nadi produces nadi dosha. Classical texts describe specific conditions under which nadi dosha and bhakoot dosha are considered cancelled. A careful practitioner treats the Guna score as one input among several, and examines the 7th house, Venus, the Navamsa and the Dasha periods of both charts before offering a view, rather than reducing a marriage to a number out of 36.

## Manglik or Kuja dosha
Manglik dosha is said to arise when Mars occupies the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th or 12th house, counted from the Ascendant, the Moon or Venus depending on the tradition followed. It is traditionally associated with friction and with mismatched temperament in marriage. Classical practice lists many cancellations, including Mars in its own or exaltation sign, Mars aspected by Jupiter, and both partners carrying the same condition. The practical position taken by careful astrologers is that Manglik dosha is widely over-applied and rarely decisive on its own, and it should never be presented to a person as a barrier to marriage.

## Difficulties in an existing relationship
When a person asks about strain in a current relationship, the reading examines the 7th house and its lord for the partnership itself, the 6th house for conflict and disputes, the 4th house and the Moon for the person's own emotional state, and the running Dasha for whether the difficulty is tied to a period that will pass. A difficulty arising during an Antardasha of a planet afflicting the 7th is read as time-bound; one shown by the natal chart's fixed structure is read as a matter of ongoing adjustment.

## What a relationship reading should and should not claim
A relationship reading describes tendencies, timing windows and areas needing attention. It cannot name a person, guarantee that a specific relationship will or will not last, or determine anyone's choices for them. A responsible reading avoids fatalistic statements about marriage, avoids telling a person that a relationship is doomed, and is explicit that the chart indicates conditions rather than outcomes.
